# BahiKhata.com — research and concept

Prepared November 2026. Benchmarks are from live visits to 38 sites in the khata, billing, accounting, tax, lending, calculator and accounting-education niches.

## 1. The decision

**Build BahiKhata.com as a free, no-install digital bahikhata (browser app) surrounded by Indian small-business money tools, plain-language guides, and an expert-help desk that sells qualified leads to CAs, GST practitioners and lenders.**

| Option | Traffic potential | Monetisation | Build cost | Moat | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| History/culture blog about bahikhata | Low | AdSense only, thin | Low | None | Reject |
| Full cloud accounting SaaS | High | Subscriptions | Very high (cloud, GST APIs, support) | Crowded: Khatabook, OkCredit, Vyapar, Tally, Zoho | Not now |
| E-commerce for paper bahi books | Niche, seasonal (Diwali) | Product margin | Inventory, logistics | Low | Reject |
| **Web khata + tool suite + lead-gen desk** | **High (khata, GST, invoice, byaj, EMI queries)** | **AdSense + leads + sponsorship + support** | **Static site, free hosting** | **Exact-match generic domain, no-install, privacy, Hindi** | **Pick** |

Why it wins:
1. Every big competitor (Khatabook, OkCredit, Vyapar, myBillBook) funnels users into an **app install and phone-number signup**. A browser khata that works instantly, offline, with data kept on the device, captures the "use it right now" intent the apps leave on the table.
2. Calculators and generators (GST, invoice, byaj, EMI, amount in words, GSTIN) are evergreen, high-volume searches with commercial intent, ideal AdSense inventory.
3. The people using these tools are exactly the people who need a CA, GST registration or a working-capital loan. **Lead generation is the real business; AdSense is the floor.**
4. Every WhatsApp reminder sent from the khata carries a "Sent with BahiKhata.com" line: a built-in viral loop into the customers of every shop that uses it.

## 2. Revenue model (planning assumptions, not data)

Assumptions for a month-12 target of 150,000 pageviews and 60,000 users a month. Replace with real figures from AdSense and the lead inbox as they arrive.

| Stream | Assumption | Monthly range |
|---|---|---|
| AdSense | ₹60–₹200 RPM on India-heavy finance/business traffic | ₹9,000 – ₹30,000 |
| Expert-help leads | 0.6% of users submit (≈360 leads) × ₹200–₹600 per lead paid by partners | ₹72,000 – ₹2,16,000 |
| Sponsorships | 2 tool/guide sponsors × ₹15,000–₹50,000 | ₹30,000 – ₹1,00,000 |
| Support pledges | Small, mostly diaspora and loyal users | ₹2,000 – ₹10,000 |
| **Total** | | **≈ ₹1.1 lakh – ₹3.5 lakh** |

Levers that move the number most, in order: lead conversion rate, partner price per lead, returning khata users (repeat pageviews), then RPM.

**KPIs to track weekly:** leads per 1,000 sessions; % of leads accepted by partners; returning visitors to /khata/; reminder messages sent (proxy: referral visits from wa.me); AdSense RPM by page type.

**Bold path:** add optional cloud sync (Supabase) and a paid Pro tier (₹99/month: multi-device sync, automated SMS/WhatsApp reminders, branded statements), then turn the expert desk into a transparent CA marketplace with ratings.
**Safe path:** keep the site static, grow tools and guides for SEO, sell leads to 20–50 partners city by city.

## 4. Trademark check

"Bahi khata" is a centuries-old generic Hindi/Urdu term for a ledger book. It is used descriptively by many unrelated apps (OkCredit's "Digital Udhar Bahi Khata", "Smart Bahi Khata", "My Cash Book: Udhar Bahi Khata", and others). No registered word mark for "BAHIKHATA" turned up in public searches. The site therefore:

- uses the term descriptively and says so on `/trademark/`;
- disclaims affiliation with any app or company of a similar name, with GSTN/CBIC/Income Tax, and with Google/YouTube creators;
- keeps its own brand identity (logo, design, copy) original.

Before any paid marketing at scale, run a formal search on the IP India public search in classes 9, 35, 36 and 42.

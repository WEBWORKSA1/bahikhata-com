About BahiKhata.com

Free, honest money tools for the people who run India's shops, stalls, workshops and small offices.

Why we exist

For generations, India's traders have trusted a red cloth-bound book to keep their accounts: the bahikhata. Millions of small businesses still run on that habit, often on paper, often with udhaar they struggle to collect and GST rules they find confusing.

BahiKhata.com takes the habit online without taking it away from its owner. Our digital khata works in any browser with no signup, keeps entries on the user's own device, and speaks Hindi and English. Around it we have built the calculators and generators a small business reaches for every day, and guides that explain money matters in plain language with rupee examples.

What you will find here

  • A free digital khata for customer and supplier balances, a cash book and reminders.
  • Business tools: GST, invoices, interest, EMI, margins, break-even and more.
  • Guides and videos on khata, GST, cash flow and loans.
  • Expert help from verified professionals when a calculator is not enough.

How we keep it free

We are open about how this website earns money:

  • Advertising through Google AdSense, shown around our content and never inside the khata's entry screens.
  • Referral fees from professionals in our expert network when we connect them with a business that asked for help. This never changes the fee you are quoted.
  • Sponsorships, always labelled, which never change our calculators or guides.
  • Support from readers who choose to contribute.

Independence

BahiKhata.com is not a government website and is not affiliated with any accounting app or company. "Bahi khata" is a generic term for a ledger book, and we use it in that sense. See our trademark and copyright notice and editorial policy.

Cookie policy

Last updated 12 November 2026.

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. Local storage is a similar browser feature that keeps data on your device. Here is what this site uses.

Essential storage (on your device only)

NamePurposeKept
bk-khata-v1Your digital khata books, entries and settingsUntil you delete it
bk-invoice-v1, bk-invoices-v1Your current and saved invoicesUntil you delete them
bk-themeLight or dark display preferenceUntil cleared
bk-cookie-okRemembers that you closed the cookie noticeUntil cleared
bk-utm, bk-landing, bk-ref (session)Which page or campaign brought you, attached to forms you submitUntil you close the tab

This data is not sent to us, except the last item when you choose to submit a form.

Advertising cookies

Google AdSense and its partners use cookies to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure performance and, where permitted, personalise ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage personalised ads in Google Ads Settings and learn more at Google's advertising policy page. Where the law requires it, you will be asked for consent before these cookies are used.

Embedded services

YouTube videos use privacy-enhanced mode and do not load until you press play. Google Fonts serves our typefaces. These services may log technical information when they load.

How to control cookies

You can block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings. Blocking essential storage will stop the khata and invoice generator from saving your work. Clearing site data deletes your khata, so download a backup first.

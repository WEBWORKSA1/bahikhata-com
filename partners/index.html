Join our expert partner network

Shopkeepers, traders and freelancers use BahiKhata every day to keep accounts. When they need a professional, we connect them with partners like you.

Why partner with BahiKhata

  • Intent-rich enquiries. Requests come from business owners actively using GST, invoice and loan tools, often with a deadline.
  • Matched, not broadcast. Each enquiry goes to one partner chosen by city, language and service, so you are not competing with ten others.
  • Structured details. Service needed, business type, turnover range, state, GST status and preferred call time arrive with every lead.
  • Visibility. Featured partners can appear in relevant guides and tool pages.

ProfessionTypical requests

Our standards

Partners agree to quote fees before starting work, contact enquiries only about the request made, respond within one working day, and never ask clients for payments in cash to personal accounts. Partners that receive repeated complaints are removed.

Is this service really free?

Yes, for you. Requesting a call-back and the first consultation are free. If you decide to hire the professional, they quote their fee to you directly before starting. BahiKhata.com may receive a referral fee from partner professionals; it never changes the price you are quoted.

Who are the experts?

Independent chartered accountants, GST practitioners, tax consultants, bookkeepers and lending advisors who have joined our partner network and agreed to our standards on clear pricing and respectful contact.

Will I get many sales calls?

No. We share your request with one matched professional, not a list of agents. If you are unhappy with how you were contacted, tell us through the contact page and we will act on it.

Can I get help if I have not started my business yet?

Yes. Many requests are from people planning to start: choosing a business structure, registering for GST or Udyam, and setting up books from day one.

      Common questions

      Is the BahiKhata digital khata really free?

      Yes. The khata, cash book, reports and every calculator are free to use without an account. The site is supported by advertising, sponsors, donations and our expert-help service.

      Where is my khata data stored?

      In your own browser, on your own device. We do not upload or see your entries. Download a backup file regularly from Settings so you can restore it on a new phone.

      Can I use it in Hindi?

      Yes. Switch the khata to Hindi from the language button at the top of the app. Our glossary also explains common khata and GST terms in Hindi and English.

      Does it replace accounting software?

      It replaces the paper udhaar register and cash book. If you need GST returns, inventory with batches, or multi-user access, use full accounting software; our experts can help you choose one.

      Is BahiKhata.com connected to the government or to any khata app company?

      No. We are an independent website. "Bahi khata" is a generic Hindi term for a ledger book. Read our trademark and copyright notice.

"Bahi khata" is a generic term

"Bahi khata" (बही खाता, also written bahi-khata, bahikhata or bahi khaata) is a common Hindi and Urdu expression for a traditional account book or ledger, used in Indian commerce for centuries. BahiKhata.com uses the expression in this ordinary, descriptive sense to explain what the website offers: a ledger and money tools for small businesses.

BahiKhata.com does not claim any exclusive right to the words "bahi khata" or "bahikhata" as a generic term, and does not suggest any connection with any other business, app or product that uses similar words.

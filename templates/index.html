How to use these templates

Print: opens a ruled A4 landscape sheet in a new tab with your browser's print window. Choose your printer, or "Save as PDF". Allow pop-ups for this site if nothing opens.

CSV: opens in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice with the column headings ready. In Google Sheets choose File, then Import, then Upload.

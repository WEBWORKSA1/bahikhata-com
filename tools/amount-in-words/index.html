Amount in words

Type a number and get it written in words, the Indian way in lakh and crore, or the international way in millions.

Number system
Style
In words

The Indian number system

FigureIndianInternational
1,00,000One lakhOne hundred thousand
10,00,000Ten lakhOne million
1,00,00,000One croreTen million
100,00,00,000One hundred croreOne billion

In Indian grouping, the first comma comes after three digits from the right, and every two digits after that: 12,50,075.

Writing amounts on a cheque

  • Start the words right at the beginning of the line and end with "Only".
  • Write paise as "and Fifty Paise" or leave a fraction like 50/100 as your bank prefers.
  • Draw a line through any unused space so nothing can be added.
  • Make sure the figure in the box matches the words exactly.

Questions

How do you write 1,50,000 in words?

Rupees One Lakh Fifty Thousand Only.

Is it "lakh" or "lac"?

Both spellings are common, but "lakh" is the standard spelling used by the Reserve Bank of India and most banks.

Does the invoice generator write amounts in words?

Yes. Our invoice generator adds the total in words automatically, in the currency you choose.

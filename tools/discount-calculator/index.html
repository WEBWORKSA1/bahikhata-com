Discount calculator

Check the final price after any offer, and what it does to your margin before you announce it.

Successive discounts are not added

"10% + 5% off" means 10% off the price, then 5% off the reduced price. On ₹1,000 that is ₹900, then ₹855: an effective discount of 14.5%, not 15%.

Effective discount = 1 − (1 − d1) × (1 − d2)

What a discount costs a seller

A discount comes straight out of profit. On an item with cost ₹800 and price ₹1,000, profit is ₹200. A 10% discount cuts the price to ₹900 and profit to ₹100: half the profit is gone, and you need twice as many units to earn the same. Our guide on pricing and margins has a full table.

Questions about discounts

Is GST charged before or after discount?

A discount shown on the invoice at the time of sale reduces the taxable value, so GST is charged on the discounted price.

Can I sell above MRP if I give a discount later?

No. MRP is the maximum retail price including all taxes. You can sell below MRP but not above it.

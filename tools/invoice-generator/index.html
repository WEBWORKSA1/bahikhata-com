What this generator puts on your invoice

  • Your name, address, GSTIN and state, and the customer's details and GSTIN.
  • A unique invoice number with your prefix, such as INV/26-27/1.
  • HSN or SAC, quantity, unit, rate, discount and taxable value for each item.
  • CGST and SGST for sales within your state, or IGST for sales to another state, decided from the two states you pick.
  • A rate-wise tax summary, round-off, the total in words, bank details and a UPI QR code for instant payment.

For the full list of mandatory fields, read our GST invoice format guide. If your turnover is above ₹5 crore, B2B invoices also need an IRN from the government's e-invoice portal.

Save as PDF

Click Print or save as PDF. On a computer choose "Save as PDF" as the printer. On Android choose "Save as PDF" in the print screen, and on iPhone pinch out on the preview to share the PDF.

Questions

Is this invoice valid for GST?

An invoice is valid when it contains the details required by GST rules, whatever tool made it. Fill in every field the generator asks for, use the correct HSN and rate, and report the invoice in your GSTR-1.

When should I choose bill of supply?

Use a bill of supply if you are registered under the composition scheme or are selling exempt goods or services. It shows no GST.

Can I make invoices in dollars for foreign clients?

Yes. Choose the currency at the top. GST columns are hidden for non-rupee invoices; exports of services usually need a letter of undertaking to be zero-rated, so check with your accountant.

Where are my invoices stored?

Only in your browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Save a PDF copy of every invoice you issue.
